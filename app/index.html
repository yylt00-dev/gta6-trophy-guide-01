0% to the platinum

    The platinum in three stages, built from the official trophy descriptions and PowerPyx's roadmap. Stage 1 is the story (60–80 hours); stage 2 is the 100% checklist clean-up (about 100 hours); stage 3 is the last side strands (about 10 hours). Progress bars below follow your own checklist.

    One trophy can be missed. Everything else is safe.

    PowerPyx flags a single potentially missable trophy in the whole list. The rest of the trophies are either automatic story unlocks or stay available in free-roam after the story — there is no mission replay, but nothing else locks you out.

    Save advice (PowerPyx)

    The game has 15 manual save slots plus 1 autosave slot, and no mission replay. Keep a save at the start and end of each chapter (10 saves), then rotate the remaining 5 for free-roam clean-up. And never use cheats on a trophy run: cheats disable trophies and saving.

    About this guide

    An offline, open-source guide and tracker. It loads no file from the network, stores your progress only in your own browser (localStorage), and exports it as a file you own. No Rockstar Games logo, artwork or game screenshot is used anywhere — the look is original.
